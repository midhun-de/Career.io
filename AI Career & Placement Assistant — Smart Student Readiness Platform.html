🎯 Problem Statement

Why Many College Students Struggle With Placements

6 Key Challenges Addressed in One Integrated Platform

1. Which career is suitable for them?

Students often pick tracks randomly without matching their academic strengths, interests, and coding comfort.

2. What skills companies expect?

College syllabi rarely reflect modern industry benchmarks for Full-Stack, Data Science, or Backend roles.

3. What they should learn next?

Information overload leaves students jumping between tutorials without a sequential step-by-step path.

4. Whether they are placement-ready?

Students lack an objective metric showing their current readiness percentage and weak areas before campus drives.

5. Which projects they should build?

Generic to-do apps don't impress recruiters; students need role-aligned full-stack and AI portfolio project ideas.

6. How to prepare for Technical & HR interviews?

Last-minute cramming fails without structured DSA topic checklists, CS core revision, and HR answer frameworks.
